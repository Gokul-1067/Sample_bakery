HELPFUL VISITOR INFO

Frequently asked questions.

When is the best time to get hot Kerala puffs?

Our puffs come out blisteringly hot from the ovens at 4:00 PM and again at 6:00 PM every day. Trays sell out quickly, so arriving around 3:50 PM guarantees you get them at peak flaky crispness straight from the baking trays.

Do you use vanaspati or artificial food colors?

Never. We enforce a strict zero-vanaspati policy across our entire bakery. All our items are made exclusively with 100% pure butter, slow-simmered farm ghee, cold-pressed coconut oil, and natural spices and extracts.

Can I pre-order snack boxes or custom celebration cakes?

Yes! We prepare custom snack boxes (assorted puffs, cutlets, unniyappam, and rolls) for office events, family gatherings, and weddings. For large party orders (20+ items), please give us 24 hours notice.

Can you slice my sourdough loaves on-site?

Absolutely. Our front counter has precision bread slicers and we can slice your sourdough boule or seeded loaves to your preferred sandwich or toast thickness at no additional charge.
