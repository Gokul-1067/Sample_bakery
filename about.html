CHAPTER 01

A Charcoal Hearth & Tellicherry Pepper

In 1998, on a bustling corner near Kochi's Bakery Junction, Maannappam began with a single wood-fired brick oven and a family recipe for slow-braised beef and chicken puffs.

While conventional bakeries shifted toward industrial hydrogenated fats and commercial dough improvers, our founders insisted on rolling pastry by hand with pure butter, braising meat with fresh crushed Tellicherry pepper, and serving puffs piping hot when the 4 PM bell rang.

16°C Cold Atelier
CHAPTER 02

Normandy Cultured Butter & 81 Micro-Layers

In 2012, second-generation baker Mathew returned from classic pastry apprenticeships in Normandy and Lyon with a bold aspiration: to introduce world-class European viennoiserie to Kerala without abandoning local soul.

We constructed our 16°C climate-controlled lamination room to work with high-butterfat cultured French butter. The result was a croissant that could rival the finest Parisian boulangeries—golden, shatteringly crisp outside, and delicately honeycombed within.

✨ TIMELINE OF CRAFT

From A Single Oven To A Living Institution

1998

The Wood-Fired Brick Oven Opens

Founded at Bakery Junction, Kochi. The first batch of wood-fired Malabar meat puffs and tea cakes sells out in under 45 minutes.

2008

The 4:00 PM Snack Bell Tradition

The legendary afternoon snack bell is born, signaling freshly fired puffs, cutlets, and piping-hot tea for Kochi's commuters and regulars.

2016

The 16°C French Lamination Room

Commissioned an imported cold lamination atelier to craft authentic 81-layer croissants, pain au chocolat, and seasonal fruit danishes.

Today

150+ Daily Bakes & Heritage Sourdough

Three living hearths firing from 4:30 AM to 10:00 PM every day, serving generations of families who cherish honest, handcrafted baking.
