ESTD 1998 KOCHI

CHAPTER 01 • OUR ROOTS

It all began with a wood-fired stone oven at Bakery Junction.

In 1998, Maannappam opened its doors in Kochi with a single brick oven, glowing casuarina wood embers, and an uncompromising commitment to pure ingredients. Our founder began with grandmother's handwritten spice notes: roasting whole Tellicherry black peppercorns, hand-crushing green Idukki cardamom, and slow-cooking spiced minced meat for the now-legendary evening puffs.

By 4:00 PM every afternoon, the sound of the bakery brass bell signaled that golden pazhampori was coming out of fresh cold-pressed coconut oil, and trays of bubbling curry puffs were leaving the stone hearth. It wasn't just a snack counter—it became an essential daily neighborhood ritual.

CHAPTER 02 • THE EXPEDITION

Crossing continents to master the geometry of French butter.

Two decades into our journey, our second-generation bakers travelled through traditional boulangeries and patisseries across Normandy, Paris, and Brittany. They immersed themselves in classical French viennoiserie—learning how 84% churned French butter behaves under precise 16°C temperature control, and how wild levain starters cultivate complex, honeycombed sourdough crumbs.

Instead of choosing between European patisserie and our Kerala snack heritage, we realized the greatest bakery in the world would celebrate both. We returned home, installed specialized stone-deck ovens alongside our traditional copper kadais, and unified both craft traditions under one roof.

BEHIND THE GLASS

Our three sacred kitchen ateliers.

Explore What We Bake →
🥟 01. The Kerala Kadai

Fired for the iconic 4 PM tea-time rush. Hand-cut beef, chicken, egg, and vegetable puffs layered with crispy crusts; sweet unniyappam caramelized in brass appakarams; and ripe organic Nendran bananas sizzled in wood-pressed coconut oil.

🥐 02. The Parisian Lamination Room

Chilled strictly to 16°C year-round. Our pastry artisans hand-fold imported 84% butter into slow-rested dough over three days, baking croissants, pain au chocolat, almond swirls, and fruit danishes fresh at 6:30 AM every morning.

🥖 03. The Stone Hearth Deck

Where raw flour meets glowing stone and high steam. Using our heirloom wild levain starter cultivated since 2012, rustic sourdough boules, French baguettes, and herb focaccia undergo a 72-hour slow cold fermentation with zero commercial improvers.

ZERO SHORTCUTS

The ingredients manifesto.

🧈

100% Real Butter & Ghee

We strictly prohibit vanaspati, hydrogenated palm fats, and synthetic shortening. Every single bake uses pure dairy butter and slow-simmered farm ghee.

🌾

Stone-Milled Ancient Grains

No chemical bleaching agents or artificial bread improvers. We source single-origin stone-ground wheat, rye, and whole grains for rich flavor and natural digestion.

🌶️

Direct Highland Spices

Sun-dried Tellicherry black peppercorns, crushed green Idukki cardamom, and Wayanad ginger sourced directly from Kerala family-owned spice estates.

⏱️

Time as an Essential Ingredient

No rushed shortcuts. Our breads and laminated viennoiserie rest in temperature-controlled cold fermentation for up to 72 hours to unlock authentic flavor.

OUR CHRONICLE

28 years of fire, flour, & flavor.

1998

The Wood-Fired Hearth in Kochi

Founder opened Maannappam with a single brick oven, baking spiced egg puffs, coconut buns, and evening tea rolls for morning port workers and local families.

2004

The 4 PM Snack Bell Tradition

The afternoon tea-time bell became a cherished local ritual. Neighborhoods gathered daily as hot meat puffs and golden pazhampori were pulled from the oven and kadai.

2012

Cultivating the Wild Mother Levain

Began cultivating our living sourdough culture using local whole wheat and mountain spring water, starting our journey into artisan wild fermentation.

2018

The European Boulangerie Apprenticeship

Our bakers trained across Paris and Normandy, bringing back vintage French lamination techniques, stone deck ovens, and high-fat butter folding mastery.

2024

Flagship Open-Concept Bakery & Cafe

Unveiled our Kochi flagship featuring glass-walled production ateliers where patrons watch French croissant sheeting and Kerala kadai frying in real-time.

2026

Maannappam Today — 150+ Daily Bakes

Serving over a thousand guests daily with 150+ artisanal items, remaining true to our founding principle: pure ingredients, zero shortcuts, and endless love for the craft.
