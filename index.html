FOUNDED 1998 • KOCHI

Two Culinary Traditions, One Roaring Hearth.

Founded along Kochi's historic trade lanes, Maannappam began with a single wood-fired stone oven and an obsessive belief: local Kerala snacking culture deserves the exact same precision and craftsmanship as classical European baking.

Today, we bring together time-honored Malabar spices and authentic French lamination under one roof—ensuring every puff crackles and every crumb tells a story.

01 The 16°C Viennoiserie Room ↗

Imported cultured butter, 81 laminated micro-leaves, and 72-hour cold proofing for maximum honeycombed air pockets.

02 The Deck Snack Hearth ↗

Tellicherry black pepper, hand-pulled meat masala, shallot confit, and cold-pressed coconut oil fired at 220°C.

03 The Heritage Brew Bar ↗

Highland Munnar BOP cardamom chai, slow brass samovars, and citrus-spiced hot Sulaimani poured from height.

Experience The Warmth of Our Hearth.

Join us at Bakery Junction, Kochi. Walk in for the 4:00 PM snack bell or pre-order handcrafted sourdough boules and celebration cakes for your weekend celebrations.
