OUR STORY

Born from an authentic brick oven. Built for food lovers.

Maannappam started in 1998 with grandmother's recipe notebook and a wood-fired hearth. Over 28 years, our bakers journeyed from old Kerala tea stalls to European boulangeries, blending time-honored Indian snack traditions with classical French lamination.

✔ 100% Real French Butter & Ghee ✔ Zero Artificial Additives ✔ Fresh Batches Every 3 Hours
