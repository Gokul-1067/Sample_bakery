The 16°C Cold Chamber: 81 Laminated Leaves

In our strictly climate-regulated lamination atelier, marble workbenches maintain 16°C so Normandy cultured butter remains pliable without melting into the dough. Each croissant undergoes double-turns yielding 81 micro-leaves before a 72-hour cold retard.
